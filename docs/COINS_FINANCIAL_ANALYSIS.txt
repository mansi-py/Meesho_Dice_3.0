COINS & REFERRAL ECONOMICS ? REVISED TO USER MODEL
4 October 2026

CONVERSION
1 coin = Rs.1 discount/cashback value on the next eligible order.
Referrer: 30 coins = Rs.30.
Friend: 20 coins = Rs.20.
Total per successful referral: 50 coins = Rs.50.
Prototype shows use of up to Rs.50 in coins on orders Rs.299+, without stacking another promotional discount. Redemption is displayed policy; checkout application is not connected.

SLIDE-READY REFERRAL MODEL
- Referrer earns 30 coins once the friend's first eligible order is delivered and the return window closes with no return.
- The friend earns 20 coins on that same successful first order.
- Cap rewards at five successful referrals per referrer per month to limit abuse.
- Gradually reduce reward levels as trust and awareness grow, subject to measured conversion/retention results.
- At five referrals, referrer earnings total Rs.150; combined referrer/friend reward exposure totals Rs.250.

FINANCIAL RATIONALE ? USER-SUPPLIED ASSUMPTIONS
Assumed contribution LTV: Rs.178 per acquired user.
Assumed annual contribution: Rs.59 per acquired user.
Rs.50 / Rs.178 = 28.1% of contribution LTV, below 30%.
Simple payback: Rs.50 / Rs.59 * 12 = 10.2 months, below one year.
These conclusions depend on contribution-based LTV, not revenue LTV; exclude additional acquisition, fraud, moderation, technology costs and uneven contribution timing. They are not verified Meesho male-cohort economics.

OTHER REWARDS AT THE REVISED CONVERSION
Review: 10 coins = Rs.10 per eligible photo/video review.
Quiz: 3 coins per correct answer, up to 9 coins = Rs.9 for the first completed occasional quiz.
Changing from 10 coins = Rs.1 to 1 coin = Rs.1 increases those reward costs tenfold. One review plus a perfect quiz now creates Rs.19 nominal value. Stacking that with referral issuance creates Rs.69 nominal exposure per participating referral pair/user scenario; attribution and eligibility need separate analysis.
Do not use the referral-only 28.1% / 10.2-month figures to justify all stacked program rewards.

PUBLIC CONTEXT
Meesho Q4 FY26 reported marketplace contribution margin 4.0% of NMV and adjusted marketplace EBITDA -1.7% of NMV.
Source: https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MEESHO_06052026171858_MLPress_Release.pdf
These aggregate figures do not prove program affordability. Validate retained incremental orders and contribution after all rewards in a capped pilot.

PROTOTYPE LIMITS
Referral payout eligibility and the five-referral cap are displayed terms; there is no referral settlement backend. Review/quiz points update locally. Next-order redemption is not applied by checkout yet.
Do not assert a higher return rate among male users without a source; delivery/return eligibility is a proposed guardrail.
